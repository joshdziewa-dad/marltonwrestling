Before You Register

  • Confirm every date and division with the host. Dates come from current listings on WrestlingTournaments.org, FloArena, USAW-NJ and the hosts' own pages as of Oct 6, 2026. Youth flyers change, and some events cap brackets early.
  • NJ state path: USAW-NJ runs boys and girls scholastic qualifiers starting in January, leading to the state finals March 12–14, 2027. The full 2027 qualifier list isn't posted yet; last season had nine, including one at Washington Township HS (Sewell). Every wrestler needs a current USA Wrestling card.
  • League events: our SJWL duals and league tournaments are on the Our SJWL Schedule tab. The SJMSWL tournament is for middle-school league teams.
  • Residency-restricted, not listed: PJW Youth States (PA, Mar 19–21) and the MSWA Maryland Folkstyle States (Feb 14) are limited to in-state wrestlers who went through their qualifiers.
  • “Girls divisions” means the host lists separate youth girls brackets (for example Girls K–8). Some events also run high school and open brackets on the same day; register in the youth divisions.
  • Left off as high school only: Escape the Rock, Abington Friends Girls Jamboree, Garden State Classic (Peddie), Phil Carbonetta, Halloween Havoc girls, Carlo Ritacco girls, Bombers Blizzard Brawl girls, Rapid Fire Round Robin, Scott Stevens, Berkeley Bash, Ken Berlett and Davison Girls Classic.
  • Drive times are estimates from Cherokee High School (Marlton) without traffic.
